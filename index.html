Import from Roblox

Roblox won't let any outside app read your balance directly — that needs your account login cookie, which you should never hand to anything. Instead, export your transactions from Roblox and load the file here. Repeat imports only add what's new.

  1. On roblox.com, open Settings → Transactions (or Creator Dashboard → Transactions).
  2. Pick your date range and tap Export — Roblox emails or downloads a CSV.
  3. Come back here and tap the button below, then pick that file.
